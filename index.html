Growra

A task app with a companion who grows with you.

Use it for whatever helps you: daily habits, chores, study sessions, a shopping list, or planning your day. Every task you finish counts.

On Google Play soon

Use it your way

Growra doesn't tell you how to organise your life. Pick from ready-made tasks or write your own.

Custom tasks

Anything you want to track, with optional due days, repeats, priorities and colours.

Focus timers

Start a timer on any task and get a gentle alert when it's done.

Calendar

A month view of what's coming up and what you've finished.

A companion that grows with you

Your companion gains Bond as you use Growra and evolves twice. New companions join when you use the app in their favourite style.

A road made of your days

Every day you finish a task adds a tile to your Journey. Rest at camps, look back on your week, and decorate them with things your companion finds.
