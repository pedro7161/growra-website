Growra privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Growra is a task app with a companion game. This page explains what data the app handles and why. In short: your tasks and progress stay on your phone, purchases are handled by Google Play, and the optional rewarded ads come from Google AdMob.

Your tasks and progress

Everything you create in Growra (tasks, completions, companions, your Journey, settings) is saved only on your device. Growra has no account system and no server, and we never receive or store your tasks. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted unless you made a backup code or exported a file yourself.

Purchases (Growra Plus)

Growra Plus is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Google Play. Google handles your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy. The app only asks Google Play whether your account owns Growra Plus, so it can unlock the Plus features and restore them after a reinstall.

Optional rewarded ads

Free users can choose to watch a short ad to send their companion exploring. These ads are served by Google AdMob.

  • Ads are only shown when you tap the explore button. There are no banners and no ads between screens.
  • AdMob may collect device information and the advertising ID to show and measure ads. See how Google uses data in ads.
  • In the European Economic Area and the UK, Growra asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice from the same form.
  • Ads are never requested for Growra Plus users.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No access to your contacts, location or photos. Growra only reads a sound file if you choose a custom timer sound.

Children

Growra isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If the way Growra handles data changes, this page is updated and the date above changes.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the developer contact on Growra's Google Play Store listing.
